Our Story

Where Expertise Meets Purpose

Sadguru Consultancy & Services was born in 2005 from a singular conviction: that businesses deserve better than guesswork when it comes to indirect tax compliance. What began as a one-man practice in Goregaon, Mumbai, has grown into a trusted advisory firm serving hundreds of businesses across India.

Our founder, Shri Rajan Mashelkar, recognised a critical gap in the market. Companies were struggling to navigate the labyrinth of Central Excise, Customs, and Service Tax regulations — often receiving generic advice that failed to address the nuances of their specific situations. Having spent over two decades inside the system, he understood not just the letter of the law, but the spirit behind it.

That insider perspective became our competitive edge. Over the years, Sadguru has expanded its expertise to encompass the full spectrum of indirect taxation — from the transition to GST in 2017 to specialized EOU, DGFT, and customs advisory. Today, we serve manufacturers, exporters, traders, service providers, and hospitality businesses with the same founding commitment: reliable, practical, and result-oriented consultancy.

Shri Rajan Mashelkar
Founder & Principal Consultant

With over two decades of distinguished service in the Central Excise, Customs, and Service Tax departments, Shri Rajan Mashelkar retired as Superintendent of Central Excise. His extensive field experience encompasses the full range of departmental proceedings — from adjudications and personal hearings to search and seizure operations, audit investigations, and appellate representations. Having personally handled thousands of cases involving show cause notices, duty assessments, refund applications, and compliance audits, he brings an unmatched depth of practical knowledge to every client engagement.

After opting for voluntary retirement from government service, Shri Mashelkar chose to channel his expertise into consultancy — a decision driven by the belief that businesses benefit most from advisors who truly understand how the system works from the inside. His qualifications enable him to represent clients before adjudicating authorities, Commissioner (Appeals), and CESTAT, making him one of the few consultants in Mumbai with both the authority and the experience to handle matters at every level of the appellate chain. Under his leadership, Sadguru Consultancy has successfully resolved over 1,500 cases, earning a reputation for meticulous preparation, strategic thinking, and unwavering integrity.

Akshay Mashelkar
Partner & GST Specialist

Akshay Mashelkar leads the firm's modern practice with a sharp focus on GST compliance strategy, regulatory intelligence, and client-centric service delivery. As Partner, he is responsible for ensuring that every client receives proactive, up-to-date guidance that keeps them ahead of the regulatory curve. His expertise spans the complete GST lifecycle — from initial registration and migration to the preparation and filing of all monthly, quarterly, and annual returns.

Akshay's practice is built on three pillars: precision in compliance, clarity in communication, and speed in response. He personally oversees GST return filing for the firm's clients, ensuring that every entry is accurate, every deadline is met, and every opportunity for input tax credit optimization is captured. When clients receive notices — whether for mismatches, short payments, or audit triggers — Akshay leads the response effort with meticulously drafted replies and, when necessary, personal representation before departmental authorities. His monthly compliance monitoring service ensures that clients never face the stress of last-minute filings or unexpected regulatory changes.
