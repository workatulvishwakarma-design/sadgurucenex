Industries Served

Cross-Sector Expertise

Our indirect tax advisory spans a wide spectrum of industries, each with unique regulatory requirements and compliance challenges.

Manufacturing

Comprehensive indirect tax support for manufacturing units — from GST compliance and Central Excise legacy matters to export incentives and duty optimization strategies.

Hospitality

Specialized advisory for hotels, resorts, and hospitality chains — navigating GST on services, input tax credits, and multi-location compliance requirements.

Electronics

Expert guidance for electronics manufacturers and assemblers on GST classifications, Central Excise transitions, and EOU scheme benefits.

Trading

GST registration, return filing, and compliance management for trading businesses — ensuring seamless operations and regulatory adherence at every step.

Export

Strategic advisory on export benefits, duty drawbacks, SEZ compliance, DGFT schemes, and foreign trade policy to maximize international trade advantages.

Engineering

Indirect tax solutions for engineering firms covering GST on works contracts, Central Excise on manufactured goods, and customs duty on imported capital goods.
