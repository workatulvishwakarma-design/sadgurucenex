What We Cover

Comprehensive GST Service Offerings

From initial registration to complex litigation, our GST services span the full compliance lifecycle.

GST Registration & Migration

Seamless migration from earlier tax regimes (VAT, Service Tax, Central Excise) to GST, new GST registration for businesses crossing the threshold, and amendments to existing registration details including business name, address, and authorized signatories.

GST Compliance Management

Day-to-day compliance oversight including record keeping, documentation standards, invoice verification, input tax credit reconciliation, and ensuring your business adheres to all GST regulations and filing timelines without fail.

GST Returns Filing

Preparation and timely filing of all applicable GST returns — GSTR-1 (outward supplies), GSTR-3B (summary return), GSTR-9 (annual return), GSTR-9C (reconciliation statement), and any other returns specific to your business category.

GST Notices & SCN Reply

Thorough analysis of show cause notices and GST departmental communications, followed by comprehensive reply drafting with supporting legal provisions, case laws, and evidence. We also provide representation during personal hearings.

GST Appeals

Filing appeals against adverse orders, preparation of cross-objections, and professional representation before Appellate Authority, Tribunal, and Higher Courts. We build strong legal arguments backed by statutory provisions and judicial precedents.

GST Audit Support

Comprehensive pre-audit preparation including documentation review and compliance health check, handling of departmental audit proceedings, query responses, and ensuring proper documentation is maintained and presented to audit officers.

GST Refund Assistance

Preparation and filing of GST refund applications for excess input tax credit, export refunds, inverted duty structure refunds, and other eligible categories. Complete follow-up with the department, documentation support, and tracking until refund is received.
